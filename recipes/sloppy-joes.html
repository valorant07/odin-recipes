Sloppy Joes

Home

Sloppy Joes are soft burger buns filled with a flavorful mixture of ground beef, tomato sauce and spices. They are quick, messy and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 400 g canned tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • 4 burger buns

Steps

  1. Chop the onion and bell pepper.
  2. Cook them in a large pan for a few minutes.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Add the tomatoes, ketchup, mustard and spices.
  5. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  6. Toast the burger buns.
  7. Spoon the beef mixture onto the buns.
  8. Serve immediately.
