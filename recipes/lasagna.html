Lasagna

Description

A classic lasagna made with layers of pasta, rich tomato and beef sauce, and creamy cheese. A simple and comforting family meal.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 400 g canned tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250 g lasagna sheets
  • 200 g mozzarella
  • 100 g grated Parmesan
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Steps

  1. Chop the onion and garlic.
  2. 1 onion
  3. Cook the onion and garlic in a large pan.
  4. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste and oregano.
  6. Simmer the sauce for 15–20 minutes.
  7. Layer the sauce, lasagna sheets and cheese in a baking dish.
  8. Bake at 180°C for about 40 minutes.
  9. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving.
