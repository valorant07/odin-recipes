Pasta Salad

Description

A simple and fresh pasta salad with vegetables, herbs and a light dressing. It is easy to prepare and works well as a quick lunch or side dish.

Ingredients

  • 300 g pasta
  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 100 g olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Drain the pasta and let it cool.
  3. Chop the vegetables and olives.
  4. Add everything to a large bowl.
  5. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice and oregano.
  6. Pour the dressing over the salad.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Mix well and serve.
